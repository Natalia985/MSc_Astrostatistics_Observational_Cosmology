# Assessment

- Weekly lab checkpoints: **20%**
- Six graded lab extensions: **30%** (5% each)
- Final real-data cosmology project: **40%**
- Oral scientific defence: **10%**

## Why labs and homeworks are merged

On six selected weeks, the weekly lab notebook contains:

### In-class Core Lab
Completed during the 4-hour practical and used for the weekly checkpoint.

### Extended Analysis
Completed after the practical. It is the graded homework component.

Students submit only one notebook.

## Common graded-lab rubric

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Scientific formulation | 20% |
| Statistical correctness | 25% |
| Uncertainty quantification | 20% |
| Diagnostics / failure analysis | 15% |
| Reproducibility | 10% |
| Scientific interpretation | 10% |

## Five required statements

1. **Estimand**
2. **Observed data**
3. **Generative assumptions**
4. **Uncertainty / selection**
5. **Validation / falsification test**

A high-accuracy result obtained with leakage, invalid covariance treatment, inappropriate selection, or unsupported interpretation is not a high-quality result.
