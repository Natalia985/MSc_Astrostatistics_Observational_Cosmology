# Final Project — Real-Data Observational Cosmology

**Weight:** 40%  
**Oral defence:** separate 10%

## Goal

Construct and defend an end-to-end cosmological inference using real observations.

## Required components

### 1. Supernova cosmology
Use Pantheon+ with documented covariance treatment.

### 2. AGN/QSO component
Choose one:
- SDSS-RM time-lag analysis;
- SDSS/QSO population-selection analysis based on the course paper;
- another instructor-approved real AGN dataset.

### 3. Multi-probe inference
Combine at least two observational probes.

### 4. Robustness
Repeat the analysis under one meaningful alternative:
- sample cut;
- covariance choice;
- prior;
- lag method;
- AGN selection criterion;
- probe subset.

## Required figures

1. data/generative-model diagram;
2. Hubble diagram or AGN light curve;
3. posterior/likelihood figure;
4. residual or calibration diagnostic;
5. robustness comparison;
6. one failure or limitation case.

## Final written conclusion

Maximum 1,000 words.

Separate clearly:
- what was directly observed;
- what was inferred;
- what was externally calibrated;
- what assumptions were required;
- what remains uncertain.

## Oral defence

Suggested format: 8 min presentation + 4 min questions.
